How a name becomes a tune: each syllable is one note. The vowel picks the pitch ("oo" low, "ah" in the middle, "ee" high), the consonant before it nudges it up or down, a hard K or T adds a pluck and an S adds a sparkle. Every tune comes home to the same low C. Names that sound alike punch the same holes.
